# Implementation Plan: AI Tutor Answer Sharing & Daily Streak Completion Share + Automatic Review

## 1. Overview & Goals
We will enhance **StudyWeb** with two cohesive sharing experiences while strictly preserving all existing functionality, layouts, themes, and navigation:

1. **AI Tutor Answer Sharing (`WorkspaceChat.tsx` & `AITutorWorkspace.tsx`)**:
   - Add a **Share** action button alongside existing message actions (`Listen`, `Copy`) on every AI Tutor response.
   - Clicking **Share** opens a clean, responsive **In-App Share Modal with a Preview Card** displaying the AI Tutor response excerpt, workspace context, and StudyWeb branding.
   - Automatically append the attribution and application link to all shared content:
     > *"This has been generated by StudyWeb. You can check this out: https://ais-pre-2obuey6titswgf7h6gnrum-1015831379211.asia-southeast1.run.app"*

2. **Daily Streak Completion Full-Screen Animation, Share & Automatic Review (`StreakCelebrationModal.tsx`)**:
   - Update the full-screen streak completion celebration so that when a user completes today's streak, the headline and banner clearly celebrate: **"You have completed today's streak!"**
   - After the full-screen flame and counter animation finishes (`stage === 'complete'`), display a prominent **Share Streak** CTA button alongside the existing **Continue Learning** action (in addition to the top-right share icon).
   - Clicking **Share** opens the **In-App Share Modal with a Streak Preview Card** pre-populated with:
     - Streak completion announcement: *"First of all, I have completed today's streak! 🔥 ({streakCount}-Day Study Streak on StudyWeb)"*
     - Automatic positive StudyWeb review message: *"StudyWeb has transformed my daily study routine with its AI Tutor and focus tools — 5/5 ⭐ Highly recommended for students!"*
     - Automatic referral link: *"You can check it out from here: https://ais-pre-2obuey6titswgf7h6gnrum-1015831379211.asia-southeast1.run.app"*

## 2. Architecture & Component Design

### A. Reusable In-App Share Modal (`src/components/Dashboard/ShareContentModal.tsx`)
A dedicated, portal-mounted (`createPortal` to `document.body`, `z-[100000]`) modal component that supports two modes (`type: 'ai-answer' | 'streak'`) and works seamlessly on both mobile and desktop:
- **Visual Preview Card**:
  - **AI Tutor Mode**: Shows the StudyWeb AI Tutor badge, workspace subject tag, formatted excerpt of the AI's answer, and the automatic *"Generated by StudyWeb — Check it out"* footer badge.
  - **Streak Completion Mode**: Shows an animated flame badge, the `{streakCount}-Day Streak` count, *"You have completed today's streak!"*, the automatic 5-star review quote, and the StudyWeb link badge.
- **Editable / Copyable Share Payload**:
  - Displays the full formatted share text (including the required attribution/review and StudyWeb link) so the user can see exactly what is being shared.
- **One-Click Share Actions**:
  - **Copy Message & Link**: Copies the complete formatted message + URL to the clipboard with instant visual confirmation (`Copied!`).
  - **Share via Device (`navigator.share`)**: Triggers native OS/mobile share sheet when supported by the browser.
  - **Quick Social Share Links**: Direct share buttons for WhatsApp, X (Twitter), LinkedIn, and Email pre-filled with the formatted text and StudyWeb URL.

### B. AI Tutor Answer Share Integration
- **`src/components/Dashboard/WorkspaceChat.tsx`**:
  - Add a `Share` button (`Share2` icon from `lucide-react`) next to the `Listen` and `Copy` buttons in the AI message action bar.
  - Clicking `Share` opens `ShareContentModal` with the AI answer text, workspace name, and automatic StudyWeb attribution + link.
- **`src/components/Dashboard/AITutorWorkspace.tsx`**:
  - Add a matching `Share` button next to the `Listen` button on AI Tutor responses so both AI Tutor entry points provide identical sharing functionality.

### C. Streak Completion Full-Screen Celebration & Share (`src/components/Dashboard/StreakCelebrationModal.tsx`)
- **Full-Screen Completion Copy**:
  - Update the completion message during the full-screen celebration when `activeToday` is true to prominently display **"You have completed today's streak!"** along with the daily study target detail.
- **Post-Animation Share CTA**:
  - In the bottom action bar (revealed when the full-screen animation reaches `complete`), place a prominent **Share Streak** button right next to **Continue Learning**, as well as wiring the header Share button to open the new `ShareContentModal` in `'streak'` mode.
  - Pre-populate the share message with:
    1. *"First of all, I have completed today's streak! 🔥 ({streakCount}-Day Study Streak)"*
    2. Automatic positive review: *"My review of StudyWeb: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ StudyWeb makes staying consistent with daily study goals and AI-assisted learning effortless!"*
    3. *"You can check it out from here: <StudyWeb App URL>"*
